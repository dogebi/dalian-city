# Dalian City

An interactive voxel interpretation of Dalian, inspired by photographs of [Xinghai Square](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Xinghai_Square_(Dalian)), [Xinghai Bay Bridge](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Xinghai_Bay_Bridge), and [Zhongshan Square](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Zhongshan_Square_(Dalian)). The scene compresses and rearranges landmarks and is not a geographic or architectural survey. Other buildings are fictional.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Three.js loads from a CDN and requires internet access. Drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and click buildings to see their descriptions.
